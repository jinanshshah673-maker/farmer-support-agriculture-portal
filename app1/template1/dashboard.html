Best Practices Checklist
  • Soil Health Testing: Test soil N-P-K and pH once every 2 years through the Soil Health Card Scheme.
  • Seed Treatment: Always treat seeds with biofertilizers (Rhizobium / Azotobacter) before sowing.
  • Micro-Irrigation: Utilize Drip or Sprinkler systems under PMKSY to save up to 40% irrigation water.
  • Recycle Crop Stubble: Convert straw and stubble into compost rather than burning to conserve soil moisture.
